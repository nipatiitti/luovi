## 1. Sailing-season statistics (measured)

**Data.** Finnish Meteorological Institute open data, WFS stored query `fmi::observations::wave::timevaluepair` (siblings `::simple` and `::multipointcoverage`), parameters `WaveHs` (significant wave height, m, given at 0.1 m resolution), `WTP` (modal, i.e. peak, period, s), `ModalWDi` (peak direction), 30 min step, at most 168 h per request, no API key. Queried by bounding box 17 to 31° E, 58 to 66° N, which returns five buoys: Helsinki Suomenlinna (fmisid 103976), Pohjois-Itämeri / Northern Baltic Proper (134220), Suomenlahti / Gulf of Finland (134221), Selkämeri / Bothnian Sea (134246), Perämeri / Bothnian Bay (137228). The Harmaja, inner Helsinki archipelago and Archipelago Sea buoys of [BJ19] are not in the open data. Wind from `fmi::observations::weather::timevaluepair`, `ws_10min` at the 30 min step, at Kalbådagrund (101022, paired with the gulf buoy, about 20 km east), Harmaja (100996, 2 km from Suomenlinna), Utö (100908, 60 km NE of the Baltic Proper buoy), Kylmäpihlaja (101061, 90 km SE of the Bothnian Sea buoy) and Fagerholm (100924, central Archipelago Sea). 2016 to 2025, 1 May to 30 September, about 1 400 to 1 500 buoy-days per site. The buoys are in the water all season (sample counts per season are near the 7 344 maximum except Baltic Proper 2016, 2017 and 2022).

**Joint Tp.** For each Hs percentile, Tp is the median (with P25 to P75) of the peak periods measured at that Hs (±0.05 m, i.e. the 0.1 m class), not the Tp percentile. Lp and cp are from the finite-depth dispersion relation at the buoy depth (22, 62, 100, 120 m [BJ19, BJ18, BJ20]). Wind is the median station wind in the same samples, at the anemometer height: Kalbådagrund 31.8 m and Harmaja 17.5 m [BJ19, via the earlier note]; Utö and Kylmäpihlaja heights not found. A neutral log profile with z0 = 0.2 mm gives U10 ≈ 0.90 U(31.8 m) and 0.95 U(17.5 m) [M]; stable summer air would lower U10 further.

## 3. Reproducing measured pairs with the build's inputs (computed)

Method [M]: the build's spectrum parameters recomputed with `build.ts`'s formula from `data`'s `jonswap` and `piersonMoskowitz` tables, Hs from the trapezoid integral of the compiled `frequencyDensity` (JONSWAP-TMA) to 20 ω_p, Tp = 2π/ω_p, Lp from `dispersionWavenumber`; every row below re-evaluated through the compiled `buildSea` (identical to 4 significant figures; the earlier note's three seas reproduce exactly). For each pair: (a) the wind and fetch that give both Hs and Tp, if any; (b) with the measured wind (reduced to 10 m where the height is known), the fetch that matches Hs; (c) the least Hs the build can make at that Tp (the Pierson-Moskowitz-capped sea).

**Why the median pairs fail.** At a given Tp the build's Hs is bounded below by the fully developed sea: at γ = 1 that is Hs/Lp ≈ 0.027, at γ = 3.3 about 0.033. Measured median-day steepness is 0.013 (Suomenlinna) to 0.024. So no wind, fetch, depth and γ the contract allows reproduces a median summer pair. The measured peak in light air belongs to waves older or longer than the local wind sea: attenuated gulf waves at Suomenlinna [BJ19 §2.4], waves outlasting a dropping wind, or waves from the Baltic Proper. One JONSWAP-TMA system cannot hold both the local short sea and that longer, lower one (the "one wave system per scenario" decision in the earlier note).

## 5. Spectral shape parameters

- Kahma 1981 (Bothnian Sea buoys, 1976 and 1979): saturation range S(ω) = α_u U₁₀ g ω⁻⁴ with α_u = 4.5 × 10⁻³, independent of dimensionless fetch, at high dimensionless frequency. Read only as a search-engine rendering of the abstract (second-hand); the paper itself returned 403.
- Pettersson's thesis, as quoted in search snippets: in the Gulf of Finland the peak frequency changed less than the energy with fetch and the peakedness of the frequency spectrum decreased; no numbers reached.
- The FMI open data carry Hs, Tp, peak direction, directional deviation and water temperature, not spectra, so γ and peak width cannot be computed from them. The wide Tp spread inside each Hs class (P25 to P75 over 1 to 2 s at the median) is consistent with the broad, often two-system spectra of [BJ19], but is not a shape measurement.

## Not found or not reached

- Archipelago Sea and Harmaja wave buoy data: not in the FMI open data; no published Archipelago Sea seasonal statistics reached.
- Published May to September statistics (Tuomi et al. 2011, Kahma et al. 2003 MERI report, Soomere and Räämet 2011, HELCOM fact sheets): only annual or narrative text reached, so the seasonal numbers above are this note's own computation from measured data, not checked against a published seasonal table.
- Kahma 2021 Geophysica review: certificate failure. Pettersson 2004 thesis, Pettersson et al. 2010 and Tuomi et al. 2012 PDFs at Ifremer, Kahma 1981 at AMS, scilit: HTTP 403.
- Anemometer heights at Utö, Kylmäpihlaja and Fagerholm; Bothnian Bay buoy depth.
- Any measured Baltic γ, peak width σ or a seasonal stability breakdown.

## Reproducing

The scripts were written for this note and are not committed. `download.sh` pages the two stored queries in 168 h windows with retries; `parse.py` extracts the time-value pairs with a regular expression (run with `python3 -I`); `analyze.py` builds tables 1 and 2; `exceed.py` counts the time at or above each reference sea; `invert.mjs` builds table 3 and the reference-sea rows by importing the repo's compiled `sim-ocean` and `data` packages.
